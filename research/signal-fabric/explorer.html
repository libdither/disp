Ways a self-configuring fabric could carry a program's signals, costed from one device model (a 45–28 nm-like process at room temperature): the energy one run takes, how long it takes, and how often it comes out wrong. Every design must give a wrong output at most once in 1012 runs and be buildable: a fixed chip must yield (no faulty gate site among 107); a self-configuring array tests its cells and routes around the faulty ones, at the cost the routing simulation measured.
