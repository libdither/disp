Anna's Archive tee

A canvas painting made only of brush strokes. Your browser paints it live from a hidden underdrawing, big brushes first, then finer ones where the detail is.

What's in it: Anna reads Fahrenheit 451, the top pick on the "Recommended" shelf (and the book HTTP status 451, "Unavailable For Legal Reasons", is named after). Behind her glows the map of every ISBN from Anna's Archive's ISBN visualization, with her book pinned at 978-1-4516-7331-9. GPTBot, CCBot and ClaudeBot hoover up data, Monero-chan holds an XMR coin, and the ribbon lists the domains Wikipedia gives as active. Her colours are the site's own: #0096ff, #0160a7, #a0d7ff, #ff005b, #7f01ff, #fffe92.
