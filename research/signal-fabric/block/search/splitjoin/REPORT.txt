splitjoin family: can map run its parts at once?
Reproduce, from this directory, each under a memory cap and timeout:
  laws: (from ..) python laws.py splitjoin/design.py
  speed: python speed.py 7
  wave: SPLITJOIN=wave SPLITJOIN_LAY=equal python wave_bench.py 8 200000 (or SPLITJOIN_LAY=free)
  recurrence: SPLITJOIN=wave SPLITJOIN_LAY=free python checks.py

Counts: walker 130 reactions; any-fix-is-deep-swap trigger 12 (not a real fix, wrong on every other fix); sweep 12; wave 8 in place of sweep.

No-go. The rule is an involution per phase, so a token that turns back undoes the reaction it just left, then the one before.
A region a token can't leave is a closed finite bijection: its orbit is a cycle through the moment of entry. So an echo can't park:
it un-runs its whole part until the other echo happens to arrive. Measured, wave with same-tick join, free-fall layout:
  full 1 back at the exact start grid after 74 ticks; full 2 after 198; full 3 after 1498; F(L,F(L,L)) after 82, its swap made and unmade.
Merging two tokens into one anywhere but a same-tick join loses who came first; the image of a merge on a wire equals the image of a token
passing that site, so the block rule is not one-to-one, and a finite mark can't absorb a new bit per run.

Tagging is a second problem behind this one and was not built: trains or round-robin solve addressing, not the join.
