Reflective nets: the icons

Each kind of agent, what it really does, and four ways to draw it. Click one to use it in the player; the choice is kept in this browser. Every drawing has the same pins, one per port, on the same sides: only the body, the letter and the colour change. The white dot is the main port, where rewrites happen. The small picture shows the icon turned and flipped, as the layout may draw it: its letter stays upright.
